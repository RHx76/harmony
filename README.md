## Problem Statement 📔
As night falls in the VR war zone, "Battle Beats" transforms gameplay into an electrifying dance party. Players mix music and unleash their best moves, creating an intoxicating blend of rhythm and
camaraderie. Even the most reserved warriors find valor, dancing freely under vibrant lights, celebrating their triumphs together

## Built Using
- caffine and *JavaFX*
## Features
🎵 **Playback Controls**: Enjoy essential features like Play, Pause, Stop, and Shuffle for seamless listening. Easily navigate your music with Skip and Reset options.  

⚡ **Speed Control**: Adjust playback speed to enhance your audio experience. Tailor the tempo to suit your mood or preference.  

🎛️ **Equalizer**: Customize your sound with adjustable bass, treble, and other audio properties. Create the perfect audio profile for your favorite tracks.  

📂 **Playlist Management**: Create and organize playlists to keep your music collection tidy. Effortlessly manage your favorite songs and albums.  

📝 **Metadata Extraction**: Extract and display important information from MP3 files. View details like song title, artist, album, and cover image.
